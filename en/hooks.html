33 openings with a clear payoff

33 original examples in Portuguese. They are writing options, not proof of reach. Adapt the line and create the matching payoff. Do not claim you did something you have not done.

Result first

01. An idea for gathering the pencils on your desk.

What to deliver: Show the pencils together and explain the container.

02. See where I put this card.

What to deliver: Reveal the card in a suitable envelope.

03. This small space can hold loose papers.

What to deliver: Show a tray and its size limits.

Everyday question

04. Do your pencils get scattered too?

What to deliver: Answer with a concrete way to organize them.

05. Where does the card go after you read the message?

What to deliver: Show one storage option.

06. Which paper do you need to find first?

What to deliver: Sort papers by a defined need.

A detail that matters

07. Before using the jar, check this rim.

What to deliver: Show an integrity check, without claiming a generated object is safe.

08. This fold changes the envelope space.

What to deliver: Explain the fold in the example shown.

09. The base size matters for this tray.

What to deliver: Compare support and space for one specific proposal.

Honest comparison

10. Two ways to present the same jar.

What to deliver: Show the framing and explain what changes.

11. Open or closed envelope: what do you need to show?

What to deliver: Compare the purpose of each image.

12. More empty space or more objects in this photo?

What to deliver: Explain the visual choice without promising reach.

Behind the scenes

13. Here is how I chose the first shot.

What to deliver: Show the decision and why you made it.

14. This image was made with AI; see what I reviewed.

What to deliver: Identify the source and criteria you actually checked.

15. The first line changed after this read-through.

What to deliver: Show both versions you made.

A useful limit

16. A jar helps here, but it does not solve everything.

What to deliver: Show the use and one specific limitation.

17. This envelope is an idea for a small card.

What to deliver: Set a limit for the example; do not claim it works for everything.

18. A paper tray needs careful handling.

What to deliver: Explain material limits and do not invent a test.

A specific step

19. Start by choosing which pencils stay on the desk.

What to deliver: Show one selection criterion.

20. Set the card aside before planning the envelope.

What to deliver: Explain how the contents guide the size.

21. Decide where the tray will go before filling the desk.

What to deliver: Show how you plan the space.

Curiosity answered

22. What fits in this envelope?

What to deliver: Reveal the specific card and the example’s limit.

23. Why did I leave this space empty in the photo?

What to deliver: Explain reading space or visual breathing room.

24. Which opening shows the idea best?

What to deliver: Compare clarity without pretending to test with an audience.

One change

25. I changed only the framing; compare how it reads.

What to deliver: Show before and after with conditions identified.

26. I removed one line to give the explanation room.

What to deliver: Present the script versions.

27. I changed the background but kept the object.

What to deliver: Show the comparison and acknowledge unintended changes.

An invitation to observe

28. Notice where your materials pile up most.

What to deliver: Suggest observing your own desk without collecting personal photos.

29. Notice what stays hidden inside the envelope.

What to deliver: Show enough of the opening to understand the contents.

30. See if you can read this line on your phone.

What to deliver: Demonstrate text size and reading time.

A callback

31. The jar idea left an important question.

What to deliver: Answer a real question or identify it as a script question.

32. Today I’m showing the limit I left unexplained.

What to deliver: Add a truthful caution to the previous idea.

33. The next test changes only the opening.

What to deliver: Present the hypothesis and what will stay the same.
