Compare totals and rates

Fictional example: same platform, same metric, and 48 hours since each post.

VersionViewsSharesRate
A1.000202%
B500153%

Rate = shares ÷ views × 100. B has the higher rate; A has more total actions. This does not isolate a cause or predict sales.

Calculated ratio: 2%

Nonnegative whole-number counts. A zero or missing base does not produce a rate. This ratio is not the percentage of unique people and can exceed 100% if actions repeat.
