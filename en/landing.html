Before you begin

Use a social media account, phone or computer, and an editor you already know. For the AI-illustrated production, first take Image Prompting and CapCut. External tools may have their own costs and limits.

Viral System is the name of this original proposal. The course provides a work process; it does not guarantee reach, followers, or income. Images and teaching numbers are labeled as illustrations and fictional examples.

Producing the video and waiting for metrics take more time than the short practices. The deliverable requires a reviewed MP4, a checked post, and an initial metrics check. The seven-day follow-up remains pending until it happens.

Progress and the planner stay in this browser. Export copies to keep. The planner does not publish or schedule posts on social networks. The course includes reading and activities, not prerecorded video lessons.
